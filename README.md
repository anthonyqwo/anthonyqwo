## 🧑‍💻 About Me

- 🎓 M.S. student in Computer Science at **National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU)** · B.S. in CSE from **National Taiwan Ocean University**
- 🔬 My lab works on **EEG signal processing & medical image processing**
- 🦴 Researching **diffusion models + ControlNet** for pose estimation on atypical (scoliotic) skeletons
- 🏆 Competitive programmer and team main coder: ICPC, NCPC, CPE
- 🔧 Maker at heart: ESP32 / Pico / STM32, PCB design, Raspberry Pi & edge devices
- 🎮 I love building games and interactive things with Unity, Godot and Flutter

### 🌱 Right now

- 🧪 Generating synthetic scoliosis gait data (SMPL-X + motion capture) for data augmentation
- 🔔 Building **Beckonbell**, a desktop app that rings when your AI assistants are waiting on you
- 📚 Studying Operating Systems and Reinforcement Learning
